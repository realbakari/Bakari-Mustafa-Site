## Hardware

- **iMac 24" (M1)** — Primary desk machine for development, writing, and admin.
- **MacBook Air 13" (M1)** — Travel, workshops, and community events.
- **iPhone 15 Pro Max** — Daily communication, photos, and quick field notes.
- **AirPods Pro** — Calls, podcasts, and focus sessions.

## Development & hosting

- **VS Code** and terminal coding agents for day-to-day software work.
- **GitHub** for source control and open-source repositories.
- **Jekyll + Netlify** for this site—static HTML, automatic deploys from Git, and built-in form handling.
- **Resend** for transactional email delivery.
- **Safari** as my primary browser.

## Productivity & communication

- **Apple Notes & Reminders** — Quick notes, drafts, and daily task lists across devices.
- **Google Calendar & Google Drive** — Scheduling workshops, meetings, and shared documents.
- **Gmail & Outlook** — Personal, project, and organisational email.
- **Slack, WhatsApp, and Zoom** — Team coordination, community chats, and remote mentoring sessions.
- **Canva** — Event flyers, slides, and simple graphics.

## Community tools

- **[Mentors Outreach](https://mentorsoutreach.org)** — Running mentoring registrations, matching, attendance, and reporting for Homework Club.
- **[Mavuno](https://mavuno.au/)** — City spaces, groups, jobs, and mentoring for African Australians.
- **[Circle.so](https://circle.so/)** — Good option for structured cohort programs and private membership spaces.
- **WhatsApp** — Still the most reliable channel for quick coordination with diaspora and low-bandwidth communities.

## Reading & learning

- **Kindle & paperbacks** — I keep notes on what sticks in my [reading log](/reading/).
- **Podcasts & YouTube** — Commutes and technical deep dives.
