Three things grow out of the Core, the capsule at the bottom of the screen, besides the Palette. **Quick settings** holds the switches you reach for every day: press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd> or click the status marks at the capsule's right end. **Today** is the calendar and what is coming up: click the time. **Notices** are how Takumi and your apps tell you things: the capsule widens for a moment and settles back.

| | Mac | Windows | Takumi |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sound | Control Center | Quick Settings (<kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd>) | Quick settings (<kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd>) |
| Calendar | click the menu bar clock | click the taskbar clock | click the time: Today |
| Notifications | Notification Center | notifications (<kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>) | in the Core as they come; earlier ones in Today and the Palette |
| Quiet notifications | Focus | Do not disturb | **Do not disturb** in Quick settings |

## Quick settings

At the top, **Quick settings** and how the computer is powered: "Charging · 80%", "Full · plugged in", or "62% · about 3 h 10 min left". On the right, the lock and the power button.

### The switches

Each tile shows its state in words under its name. A tile that is on is tinted, with its icon in the accent colour. Click a tile to switch it. Point at it and an arrow appears at its end: click the arrow, or right-click the tile, to open the Palette on that topic with every setting it has.

| Tile | Says | A click |
|---|---|---|
| **Wi-Fi** | the network you are on, "Not connected" or "Off" | turns Wi-Fi on or off; the arrow lists networks |
| **Bluetooth** | the device connected ("AirPods and 1 more") or "Off" | turns Bluetooth on or off; the arrow lists devices |
| **Do not disturb** | "Only urgent messages", with how many are held | holds messages back (see below) |
| **Night light** | "From dusk to dawn" or "Warm all day" | warms the screen, or turns it back |
| **Stay awake** | "Until 15:04" | keeps the computer from locking or sleeping for an hour |
| **Microphone** | "On", "On · an app is listening", or "Muted · apps hear nothing" | mutes or unmutes the microphone in use |
| **Mirror** | "Each screen its own" or "The projector shows this screen"; only with two screens or more | shows your screen on the other screens too |
| **Power mode** | Balanced, Power saver or Performance | steps through the modes this computer has |

A switch that cannot work on this computer (no Bluetooth, no power modes) says "Not available" and greys out.

### Sound and brightness

The **Volume** level shows the speaker or headphones in use under it. Drag it, click along it, turn the mouse wheel over it, or use the arrow keys. Its icon mutes and unmutes. **Brightness** shows only on a screen with its own backlight, such as a laptop's. External monitors are slower to answer, so they keep their own keys and the Palette's display rows ([Displays](/takumi/docs/displays/)).

While music or a video has something playing, a card under the levels shows its cover, the title, who made it and the app, with back, play or pause and next, and a thin line along its bottom for how far along it is.

### Capture

Four buttons along the bottom. Each one closes Quick settings first, so the Core is not in the picture.

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Screenshot** | lets you drag over a part of the screen, saves it and copies it |
| **Record** | records the screen with what you chose last time; it reads **Stop** in red while recording |
| **Colour** | picks a colour from anywhere on the screen and copies its hex code |
| **Text** | lets you drag over words on the screen and copies them as text |

More in [Screenshots and recording](/takumi/docs/capture/).

### The power button

The power button turns the switches into **Stop for now**: **Sleep**, **Restart**, **Shut down** and **Log out**, each with a line saying what it does. **Sleep** locks and sleeps at once, and everything stays open. The other three close your apps, so they ask for a second press ("Press again to shut down"). Open apps are asked to close first. <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to the switches.

### Keys in Quick settings

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Moves between switches, levels and buttons |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Switches the tile |
| <kbd>→</kbd> | Opens the tile's topic in the Palette |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Closes Quick settings |
| Typing anything | Opens the Palette with what you typed |

## Today

Click the time in the capsule. Today shows the day's name and date, the week number, and the month with today in the accent colour. The month starts on the first day of the week for your language.

Page through months with the arrows on screen, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> <kbd>Page Down</kbd>, or the mouse wheel. **Today** or <kbd>Home</kbd> comes back to this month. Typing anything opens the Palette, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

Around the month, Today adds what you asked it to keep:

- **The weather**, when you turned it on, beside the day's name: "Lisbon 19° · light rain · 24° later". A click opens the Palette on `weather`.
- **Clocks for other cities**, three at most, under the date: the city, its time, "+1" when it is already tomorrow there, and how far ahead or behind it is ("+9 h", "−5 h"). A click opens the Palette on `clock`.
- **Coming up**: running timers counting down by the second, with a line that shortens as they run, then reminders with their time ("15:00", "Tomorrow 09:00", "Fri 18:00"). A click opens the Palette on them.
- **Earlier**: the last messages your apps sent, with the app and when. It makes way for the rest when space is short. "Nothing from your apps yet" when there are none.

### Timers, reminders and the weather

All three are set in the Palette, in your own words:

| Type | You get |
|---|---|
| `timer 10 tea`, `25 min timer` | **Start a 10 min timer: tea**, counting down in Today |
| `remind me in 10 min to stretch` | **Remind me in 10 min: stretch** |
| `remind me to call the bank` | asks when, in the Core |
| `remind`, `timer` | every running one; <kbd>Enter</kbd> on one cancels or stops it |
| `weather` | **Show the weather in Today**, or the weather once |
| `tokyo` | Tokyo's time, and **Enter to add to Today** |

Reminders understand "in 10 min", "in an hour", "at 3pm", "at 15:00", "tomorrow 9:00", "tonight" and the days of the week. When one rings, the Core says it even during do not disturb (you asked for it), and a press within the minute sets it again ten minutes later. A timer also plays an alarm sound when the computer has one.

The weather comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), which needs no account. It is off until you turn it on, and then asked at most every 30 minutes, only while Today or the Palette is open. More in [Language, time and Today](/takumi/docs/language-and-time/).

> **Good to know.** The clock in the capsule and on the lock screen always shows the 24-hour time for now. Today follows your choice of 12-hour or 24-hour clock.

## Notices

Takumi's own notices are short: the volume with a meter, a theme's name, a screenshot taken, a command that ran. They show for under two seconds.

A **message from an app** widens the Core with the app's icon, the message's title and its first line, for five seconds, or eight when it has buttons. The pointer resting on a notice keeps it on screen, and nothing counts down while the Core is open, so a message never runs out unseen.

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| The app's own buttons at the right end ("Reply", "Mark as read") | up to three, as the app sent them |
| **Copy 482913** | the message holds a one-time code; a click copies it without opening the app |
| **Open link** | the message has a link further down than its first line |
| A number on the app's icon | several messages from that app; the newest is showing |
| "3 more" | other apps' messages are waiting; a click brings the next |
| The notice stays | it is urgent (a battery nearly empty, a call) and waits until you put it away |

A link in the message's first line shows in the accent colour; a click opens it in the browser and puts the notice away. Only web and email links open: a message never opens a file or runs anything.

### Acting on a notice

| To | Mouse | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| Do what the message is about (often brings the app forward) | click it | <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> |
| Copy its one-time code | click **Copy …** | <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> |
| Put it away | right-click it | |
| Put every notice away | | <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Esc</kbd> |
| See the next waiting one | click "3 more" | <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Page Down</kbd> |
| Bring the one before back | | <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Page Up</kbd> |

On a Mac keyboard, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> are <kbd>Fn</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>Fn</kbd>+<kbd>↓</kbd>. Messages waiting behind others come next, newest first. One that waited a minute is old news and goes.

### Do not disturb

Turn it on with its tile in Quick settings, or type `do not disturb` in the Palette. While it is on, messages are counted instead of shown, and the capsule shows a struck-through bell with the number held. Urgent messages and your own reminders still show. Turning it off says how many came in and from which apps ("3 messages came in · From Slack and Mail · press to see them"), and a press lists them.

### Earlier messages

Every message, shown or not, is kept. Today shows the last three, and the empty Palette shows today's last three under **Earlier today**. Type `earlier` (or `notifications`, `messages`, `missed`) in the Palette for all of them, newest first, and add words to narrow them. <kbd>Enter</kbd> on one copies it. **Clear earlier messages** forgets them, and asks first.

### When an app quits unexpectedly

When one of your apps crashes, the Core says so ("Files quit unexpectedly") with **See why** and **Copy details**. **See why** shows a card in the Core: what quit and when, what happened in plain words ("It tried to use memory it does not have"), and where in its code it stopped. **Copy details** puts a full report on the clipboard, ready for a bug report or for someone helping you. Typing `crash` in the Palette finds **Why an app quit** at any time.

## From the command line

```bash
takumi switch dnd                 # do not disturb on or off
takumi switch wifi                # Wi-Fi on or off
takumi switch bluetooth           # Bluetooth on or off
takumi switch nightlight          # the night light on or off
takumi power awake 60             # stay awake for 60 minutes; "stop" ends it
takumi power profile power-saver  # or balanced, performance
takumi audio volume 30            # set the volume; up, down, mute
takumi audio mic toggle           # mute or unmute the microphone
takumi display mirror toggle      # mirror the screen on a projector, or stop
takumi timer 10 tea               # a 10-minute timer called tea
takumi timer stop                 # stop every running timer
takumi remind in 10 min stretch   # a reminder
takumi remind list                # what is waiting to ring
takumi weather on                 # show the weather in Today
takumi clock add Tokyo            # a clock for Tokyo in Today
takumi clock format 12h           # the 12-hour clock in Today
takumi debug crash                # why the last app quit
```
