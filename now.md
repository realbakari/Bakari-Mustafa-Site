## Building

**[Mavuno](https://mavuno.au/)** — Growing the social network I founded for African Australians. It's free, ad-free, and real-name, with city spaces across Australia, community groups, a verified jobs and opportunities board, and a peer mentoring directory.

**Apple apps** — Shipping and maintaining [The Message](/the-message/mac/) on Mac and [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-message-sermons-bible/id6821373129), [Enishi](/enishi/) (a private Markdown people notebook for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch), [Kora](/kora/) (community archive app for Mac), [GatherKit](/gatherkit/) (offline event check-in for Mac), [Lumen](/lumen/), and [Relova](/relova/).

**[Mentors Outreach](https://mentorsoutreach.org)** — Maintaining and expanding the mentoring program management software used by organisations to handle applications, matching, sessions, safeguarding, and reporting. Networking African-Australians runs its Homework Club on it.

**Dzaleka tools** — Supporting [Dzaleka Online](https://services.dzaleka.com/) (and its iPhone app), continuing work on [DzalekaPay](https://pay.dzaleka.com/) for local businesses and organisations in the camp, and archiving community history through Dzaleka Digital Heritage.

**Open-source tools** — Iterating on [ATO Lens](https://github.com/realbakari/ATO-Lens), [Capsule](https://github.com/realbakari/capsule), [Capsule App](https://github.com/realbakari/Capsule-App), and [UpDeck](https://github.com/realbakari/UpDeck).

## Community

**Ipswich City Council** — Serving as an external advisor on the [Multicultural Advisory Committee](https://www.ipswich.qld.gov.au/News-Articles-Folder/2026/External-advisors-to-strengthen-work-of-Multicultural-Advisory-Committee), advising council on planning, policy, and services for multicultural communities across Ipswich.

**[Networking African-Australians](https://networkingafricanaustralians.com.au/)** — Helping coordinate homework clubs across Melbourne and regional Victoria, our mental health mentoring program, and student leadership camps.
