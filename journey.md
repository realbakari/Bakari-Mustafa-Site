<div class="page-header">
  <h1>Journey</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Born in Burundi, raised in Malawi, now a founder and technologist in Brisbane.</p>
</div>

<ol class="timeline">
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">Before 2015</span>
    <p>Born in Burundi. Grew up in Malawi, where I lived in a UNHCR refugee camp.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">2015</span>
    <p>Granted asylum by the Australian government and moved to Australia.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">2016</span>
    <p>Founded Hungry Innovators, a non-profit for STEM education.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">2018</span>
    <p>Started the <a href="/projects/transition-phase-podcast/">Transition Phase Podcast</a>, interviewing Brisbane entrepreneurs and creatives about how they built their work. It ran until September 2019.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">2019</span>
    <p>Founded <a href="https://mentorsoutreach.org">Mentors Outreach</a> in Brisbane in August.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">2024</span>
    <p>Started Dzaleka Digital Heritage to archive the cultural history of Dzaleka Refugee Camp.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">2025</span>
    <p>Launched <a href="https://visit.dzaleka.com/">Visit Dzaleka</a>, guided tours of the camp led by residents trained as certified guides. In April, Networking African-Australians took 34 students to a three-day leadership camp at Mount Eliza. Dzaleka Online recorded 17,952 active users across the year.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="timeline-year">2026</span>
    <p>Released the <a href="/dzaleka-online-iphone-app/">Dzaleka Online iPhone app</a> in July. Open-sourced <a href="https://github.com/realbakari/ATO-Lens">ATO Lens</a>, <a href="https://github.com/realbakari/capsule">Capsule</a>, <a href="https://github.com/realbakari/Capsule-App">Capsule App</a>, and <a href="https://github.com/realbakari/UpDeck">UpDeck</a>. Started building <a href="https://pay.dzaleka.com/">DzalekaPay</a>. In September, Ipswich City Council <a href="https://www.ipswich.qld.gov.au/News-Articles-Folder/2026/External-advisors-to-strengthen-work-of-Multicultural-Advisory-Committee">appointed me as an external advisor</a> to its Multicultural Advisory Committee.</p>
  </li>
</ol>
