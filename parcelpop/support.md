## How to play {#how-to-play}

Parcel Pop 3D needs an iPhone or iPad with iOS {{ site.data.parcelpop.minimum_ios }} or later. It needs no account and no internet connection.

1. **Drag a parcel** from the belt into the hold. A shadow shows where it will land: green means it fits, red means it doesn't.
2. **Turn it.** Tap a parcel, or tap **Turn**, to rotate it a quarter turn before you place it.
3. **Fill every space.** When the last parcel is in, the doors close and the load is on its way.

The **Next** strip above the belt shows the parcels still to come, so you can plan ahead. Obstacles such as winches, barriers and pillars take up space in some holds; pack around them.

## Stars and scores {#stars}

Each level has a **par**: the number of moves a tidy packer needs. The moves counter shows your moves against par.

| Stars | How |
|---|---|
| ★★★ | Finish at or under par |
| ★★ | Up to 2 moves over par |
| ★ | Any finish |

Every finish passes, so a messy solution still moves you along the route. Your **score** adds points for each parcel placed, plus bonuses for perfect fits, beating par, and finishing with no hints or no undos. Each level keeps your best score, and the results card shows **NEW BEST!** when you beat it. The total at the top of the home screen is the sum of all your best scores.

## Undo, hints and restart {#help}

- **Undo** is free and unlimited. It takes back your last move, but the move still counts towards par.
- **Hint** shows a move that keeps the level solvable. You have 3 per level. If you've packed yourself into a corner, the game tells you, so you can undo or restart.
- **Restart** (the round arrow at the top) puts every parcel back on the belt.
- **Pause** (top left) has sound and haptics switches, a reminder of this load's handling rules, and Home.

Every level is checked by a solver before it ships, so every puzzle can be solved.

## Handling rules {#rules}

From level 11, some parcels carry a printed handling sticker. Each new rule is explained the first time you meet it, and the pause menu lists the rules in the current load.

| Sticker | Rule | First appears |
|---|---|---|
| Express | Must sit next to the doors, ready to unload first | Level 11 |
| Heavy | Must rest against a wall, and never next to Fragile | Level 21 |
| Do Not Turn | Fits only the way it arrives; it can't be rotated | Level 31 |
| Fragile | Keep it away from Heavy freight | Level 41 |
| Pair | Parcels with the same letter (A, B…) must touch | Level 51 |

If a placement breaks a rule, the shadow turns red and the game tells you which rule.

## Destinations {#destinations}

The route starts with vans, then box trucks, cargo boats, cargo planes and goods wagons. From level 31, warehouse racks, shipping containers and shop stockrooms join the route. Levels 15, 25, 35 and every tenth level after are marked **HARD**.

## Daily Delivery {#daily}

A new puzzle arrives each day at midnight, your local time, and it's the same puzzle for every player. Tap the round button on the Daily card to play it.

- Finishing it stamps today on your **delivery calendar**. Tap the Daily card to see the calendar, your current streak, best streak and total stamps.
- Your **streak** counts consecutive days delivered. Missing a day resets the streak but never removes the stamps you've earned.

## Game Center {#game-center}

If your device is signed in to Game Center, Parcel Pop 3D shows a welcome banner and sends your progress automatically.

- **Leaderboards:** Total Score, Levels Cleared and Daily Streak. Tap the score at the top of the home screen to open them.
- **Achievements:** 14 to collect, from loading your first van to a 30-day Daily streak.

Not signed in? Go to *Settings › Game Center* and sign in, then reopen the game. Everything you've already done is sent the next time you finish a level.

## Sound and haptics {#settings}

Turn sound and haptics on or off from the gear on the home screen, or from the pause menu during a level. The game follows your device's silent switch for sound.

## Common questions {#faq}

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Does Parcel Pop 3D have ads or in-app purchases?</summary>

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Can I play offline?</summary>

Yes. The whole game, including the Daily Delivery, works without an internet connection. Game Center scores are sent when you're next online.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>I'm stuck. Is this level actually possible?</summary>

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver. Try a **Hint**: it shows a move that keeps the level solvable. If the game says you've packed yourself into a corner, undo a few moves or restart.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Why won't this parcel turn?</summary>

It has a **Do Not Turn** sticker, so it must go in the way it arrives.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>The shadow is red but the parcel looks like it fits.</summary>

It probably breaks a handling rule, such as Express not by the doors or Heavy not against a wall. The message at the top tells you which rule.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Will my progress move to a new iPhone?</summary>

Progress is saved on your device and moves with an iCloud or computer backup when you restore to a new iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Your Game Center achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Apple either way.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>My score isn't showing on the leaderboard.</summary>

Check that you're signed in to Game Center in *Settings › Game Center*, then finish any level. Scores can take a few minutes to appear.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Does the game collect my data?</summary>

No. See the [privacy policy](/parcelpop/privacy/).
</details>

## Contact {#contact}

For help, bug reports or ideas, email [{{ site.data.parcelpop.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.parcelpop.support_email }}). Please include your device model, iOS version, the level number and what happened, and a screenshot if you can.
