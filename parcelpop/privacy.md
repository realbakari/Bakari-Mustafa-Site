  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="pp-lede">Parcel Pop 3D is a packing puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, made by Bakari Mustafa. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: <strong>Parcel Pop 3D collects no data, and your progress never leaves your device.</strong></p>

## No data collected

Parcel Pop 3D has no accounts or sign-in, and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no third-party code of any kind. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites. The developer receives nothing from the game: not your progress, not how you play, not your device details.

The privacy manifest built into the app declares that no data is collected, and the App Store label reads *Data Not Collected*.

## Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, best scores, Daily Delivery stamps and streaks, and your settings (sound and haptics) are saved only in the game's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. Like other app data, they may be included in your device's backups.

**Deleting your data.** Delete the app to remove all of its saved progress and settings.

## Game Center

If your device is signed in to Game Center, Parcel Pop 3D sends your scores to its leaderboards (Total Score, Levels Cleared and Daily Streak) and reports the achievements you earn. Game Center is Apple's service and is handled under Apple's own privacy policy ([apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)). Your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players, as with any Game Center game. The developer does not receive your Apple Account, your contacts or any other personal information.

Game Center is optional. To stop using it, sign out in *Settings › Game Center*. The game plays exactly the same without it.

## No purchases, no ads

Parcel Pop 3D has no in-app purchases and shows no advertising.

## Children

Parcel Pop 3D does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the way Parcel Pop 3D handles information ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date changed. Any future feature that sends information off your device would be optional, clearly explained in the game, and described here before it ships.
