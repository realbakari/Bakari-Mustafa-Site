## What Relova collects {#collects}

Nothing. Relova has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we never sell or share it.

## What stays on your iPhone {#on-device}

Everything you enter or capture is stored only on your iPhone, inside Relova:

- your move, tasks, addresses, budget, boxes and letters
- photos, meter readings and condition records
- documents you add, including an agent's condition report
- the people in your household

If you use iCloud backup for your iPhone, Apple includes this data in your device backup under Apple's terms. Relova itself does not upload it.

## Permissions Relova asks for {#permissions}

- **Camera.** Used to photograph rooms, meters, receipts and box contents, and to scan box labels and documents. Photos are saved inside Relova only.
- **Notifications.** Used to remind you about tasks and deadlines. These reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.

Relova uses Apple's system pickers for photos and contacts. You choose what to share, and Relova receives only the photos or the contact you pick. It does not read your photo library or address book.

## On-device processing {#processing}

Relova reads condition reports, removalist quotes, scanned documents and box labels with Apple's on-device text recognition. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Ask Relova and the quote reader use Apple's on-device language model. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

## When Relova goes online {#online}

Relova only connects to the internet when you ask it to:

- **Saving a listing.** When you paste a link to a property listing, Relova fetches that page's preview (its title and main image) from the website, the same way Messages shows a link preview. The website sees an ordinary request from your iPhone.
- **Maps and travel times.** Addresses you enter for saved listings and places that matter are looked up with Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy, to show a map and travel times.
- **Official links.** Links to government and official pages open in your browser.

## Purchases {#purchases}

Relova Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Relova receives only confirmation that the purchase was made. It never sees your payment details.

## Links to other websites {#links}

Relova links to government and official websites, such as state tenancy authorities. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Backups {#backups}

You can back up a move to a file and keep it wherever you choose, such as iCloud Drive. The file contains your records, photos and documents. Relova doesn't receive it. Anyone with the file can open it in Relova, so store it somewhere private.

## Deleting your data {#deleting}

You can delete a move and everything in it from Settings in Relova. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

## Children {#children}

Relova is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes {#changes}

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and the in-app copy.
