## About Relova {#about}

**In one line:** Relova is an iPhone app that helps Australian renters move home, from giving notice to getting the bond back.

**In a paragraph:** Relova builds a moving plan for your state, tracks the legal deadlines for updating your address, and keeps a dated, room-by-room record of the property for your bond. It also covers the bond timeline, including portable bond transfers in NSW and Victoria, letters, a budget, boxes with QR labels and the people moving with you. There's no account, records are kept on the iPhone, and Relova earns nothing from referrals. Planning and records are free; Relova Plus is a one-time purchase for PDF exports.

**Name:** Relova, written with a capital R. "Relova Plus" for the purchase.

**Made by:** Bakari Mustafa, in Australia. Relova is for iPhone with iOS {{ site.data.relova.minimum_ios }} or later.
