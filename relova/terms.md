Relova's plans, guides, deadlines, bond information and letters are **general information**, drawn from official Australian government sources. They are **not legal, financial or other professional advice**, and they don't take your particular lease or circumstances into account.

Tenancy rules differ between states and change from time to time. We work to keep Relova accurate, but we can't promise that every rule, date or calculation is complete or current. Before you rely on something, check the official page Relova links to. For advice about your situation, contact your state's tenancy authority, a tenants' advice service or a lawyer.

Letters in Relova are starting points. You decide what to send, and you send it yourself. Relova doesn't contact anyone or lodge anything for you.

*Ask Relova* answers questions from your own records using Apple's on-device language model. Its answers can be wrong, so check anything important against your records and the official source.

Relova is free to download. **Relova Plus** is an optional **one-time in-app purchase**, made through Apple's App Store. It unlocks the features described in the app at the time of purchase, such as PDF exports. There's no subscription.

If a purchase is refunded or revoked, Relova Plus locks again. You can restore a purchase on another iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.
