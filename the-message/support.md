**The Message: Sermons & Bible** is a free iPhone app for reading and listening to the sermons of William Branham and studying the Bible. For help, email [bakari@bakarimustafa.com](mailto:bakari@bakarimustafa.com). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

### Common questions

**Can I read without an internet connection?**
Yes. Download a sermon's audio or transcript from its page, and the King James Version and Berean Standard Bible are built in. Downloads are listed in *Library › Downloads*.

**How does read-along work?**
While a sermon plays, the text follows the recording word by word or paragraph by paragraph. It needs iOS 26 and Apple's on-device speech recognition, and works best with the recording downloaded. The first time, preparing a sermon can take a few minutes; it continues in the background.

**How do I move my notes to a new iPhone?**
Open *Library*, tap the *Backup & Restore* button (the drive icon), choose *Create Backup* and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, choose *Restore Backup* and pick the file. Restoring adds to what is already there; nothing is deleted.

**Where are my prayers, notes and pictures?**
In *Library*: *Prayer* at the top, then *Notes* and *My Images*. Long-press a note or verse to add labels.

**How do I turn off reminders?**
In *Library › Prayer*, tap *More › Daily Reminder*. For a reading plan, open the plan and choose *Plan Settings › Reminder*.

**Do tips unlock anything?**
No. Everything in the app is free. Tips are optional and handled by Apple. To cancel the monthly supporter subscription, open *Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions* on your iPhone. For refunds, use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Privacy

The app has no accounts or tracking, and your notes stay on your device. Read the [privacy policy](/the-message/privacy/).
